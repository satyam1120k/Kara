KĀRA: Rulebook & Piece Compendium

⚡Thrust (Buried Spear)

Top piece gains +1 extra square of movement when advancing straight forward.

🛡️Aegis (Buried Bastion)

When this tower is attacked, the attacker captures the lowest buried Bastion instead of the top piece! Attacker stays on origin.

🌀Vault (Buried Scout)

Top piece may leap over up to 1 intervening piece along a straight or diagonal ray path.

🐎Gallop (Buried Rider)

Top piece also gains the Rider’s L-shape leap (2×1 in all 8 directions).

👁️Horizon (Buried Chariot)

Top piece gains +2 range to any directional step or slide it can perform.

✨Phase (Buried Mystic)

Top piece also gains 1-step diagonal movement in all 4 diagonal directions.

Game Types

♟ Simple: move and capture only. No promotion and no stacking. Check, checkmate and Ascension still apply. Start here if you are new.

🌘 Eclipse: Simple plus Citadel promotion (rule 3 below): reach the opponent's Citadel and your piece may awaken to its Eclipse face. Still no stacking.

🏯 Towers: the full game: Eclipse promotion plus stacking, Echo traits, peeling strikes and Aegis.

1. Movement & Towers

You may move a standalone piece or the top piece of a friendly tower. Towers stack up to a maximum height of 3 tiers. Sovereigns cannot stack and cannot be stacked upon.

2. Peeling Strikes

When attacking an enemy tower of height 2 or 3 (without Aegis), you capture only the top piece into your trophies and stay on your origin square, peeling the layer!

3. Citadel Horizon Promotion

Moving into, within, or out of the opponent's Citadel (ranks 7–9 for Dawn, ranks 1–3 for Dusk) allows you to awaken that piece to its Eclipse face!

4. Ascension (Zenith Victory)

If your Sovereign occupies The Zenith (e5) and survives one full opponent turn without being placed in Check, you win by Ascension!
